FAQ

Is Wonthly free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Can I use it without periods?

Yes. If you have an IUD, are treating endometriosis, are in menopause or are pregnant, switch to "track without periods" to log symptoms, mood, medications and routines.

Is my data private?

Everything is stored encrypted on your device. If you sign in to sync, data is end-to-end encrypted so not even we can read it. No ads, no tracking, no data sales.
